# <img src="assets/icons/book-open.svg" width="28" height="28" alt=""> Wilson's Lawn Maintenance Brand Guidelines

*Draft kept ready for the business, which is free to change it. Plain-language guidance, not legal advice.*

## The marks

- **Wilson's Lawn Maintenance:** the name, with the apostrophe. Not "Wilsons" or "Wilson".
- **The logo:** a round black badge; inside it a tree over a spade, rows of mown grass below, the name on a white
  banner across the middle, and *Keeping it green* round the lower rim.
- **The tagline:** "Keeping it green."

Use the files in this repository, unmodified. Full name on first use in a piece: **Wilson's Lawn Maintenance**.

## <img src="assets/icons/circle-check.svg" width="20" height="20" alt=""> Please do

- Use the files listed in the [README](README.md), unmodified.
- Keep the badge round and whole; it already carries its own black ground, so it works on light and dark pages.
- Leave clear space around the logo, at least one eighth of its width on every side.
- Link to the business's [Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/WilsonslawnmaintenancePA/) when you share the logo online.
- Describe the business accurately: lawn care, mulch and landscaping, firewood and snow removal, out of Glen Rock, PA,
  serving York County.

## <img src="assets/icons/octagon.svg" width="20" height="20" alt=""> Please don't

- Stretch, crop, recolour, rotate, add effects, or redraw the logo.
- Show the logo on work the business did not do, or suggest it did.
- Put the logo next to another business's in a way that suggests endorsement or affiliation.
- Use the logo to suggest that the business endorses a product, an equipment brand or an event.
- Sell merchandise bearing the logo without written permission from the business.
- Pair a photo of a customer's yard with the customer's name or address. The pictures show the work, never the customer.

## Who may use the logo

- **The business's own people:** yes, for the business's work.
- **The business's customers:** yes, to say who did the work.
- **Press and educators:** yes, to refer to the business accurately.
- **Anything else** (merchandise, partnerships, commercial use): the business's permission first.
